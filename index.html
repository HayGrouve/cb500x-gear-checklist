CB500X Gear Checklist

What to check on the tag in the shop. EU standards (valid in Bulgaria), checked Nov 2026.

Minimum kit

  1. Helmet: ECE 22.06
  2. Jacket + pants: EN 17092 AA with Level 2 armour
  3. Back insert: EN 1621-2 Level 2
  4. Boots: EN 13634 with ankle protection (IPA)
  5. Gloves: EN 13594 1KP or better
  6. Earplugs

  • Helmet letters: P = full-face · P/J = modular, ride open or closed · NP = chin bar doesn't protect. Number starts with 05 = old 22.05 stock, skip it.
  • Garment class: AAA heavy · AA touring/ADV (target) · A urban, hip armour optional · B no armour · C armour carrier only. "CE" on the armour alone isn't enough.
  • Armour levels: Level 2 passes on less force than Level 1, so it protects better.
  • Boot digits: height · abrasion · cut · crush. Each is 1 or 2; 2 is better. First digit is just height (1 = short boot).
  • Gloves: KP = knuckles impact-tested. Armour without KP was never tested.

Helmet ECE 22.06

Essential
  • 22.06 tag on the chin strap
  • Fit: wear 10–15 min, firm even pressure, no forehead hot spot
  • Roll-off test: strap it, try to rotate it off forwards. It must stay on
  • Pinlock-ready visor
  • Removable, washable liner
  • Made in the last year (date on the label)
Nice to have
  • Internal sun visor
  • Emergency cheek-pad release
  • Speaker pockets for comms
  • About 1.4–1.6 kg, quiet
  • Several shell sizes · SHARP 4–5★
Luxury
  • MIPS / rotational liner
  • Carbon shell · built-in comms · photochromic visor

Full-face is safest. Modular only if marked P/J. ADV peak only if you'll ride gravel. Replace after any crash or ~5 yrs. Never buy used.

Jacket + pants EN 17092 AA

Essential
  • AA or AAA class on the garment label
  • Level 2 armour included at shoulders, elbows, hips, knees
  • Armour stays in place in a riding crouch
  • Adjustable knee-armour height
  • Zip to connect jacket to pants
  • Sleeves cover gloves, legs cover boots when seated
  • Arm and waist adjusters
Nice to have
  • Waterproof membrane
  • Large zip vents (summers top 35 °C)
  • Removable thermal liner
  • Reflective / hi-vis panels
  • Chest-protector pocket
Luxury
  • Laminated Gore-Tex (outer shell doesn't soak)
  • Airbag jacket

Textile suits a CB500X better than leather.

Extra armour EN 1621

Essential
  • Back protector, EN 1621-2 Level 2. Most jackets only have an empty pocket (~€30–80)
Nice to have
  • Swap any Level 1 armour for Level 2
  • Chest protector, EN 1621-3
Luxury
  • Airbag vest: tethered (cheaper, no charging) or electronic (needs charging, maybe a subscription)

Boots EN 13634:2017

Essential
  • Label rated x 2 2 2
  • Ankle protection (IPA)
  • Toe and heel cups
  • Stiff sole: wring it like a towel, it should barely twist
  • Oil-resistant, non-slip sole
  • Secure closure (buckles, BOA or zip + Velcro)
Nice to have
  • Tall boot (first digit 2)
  • Shin plate (IPS) · gear-shifter pad
  • Waterproof (WR)
  • Comfortable to walk in
Luxury
  • Premium ADV boots with hinged ankle braces

Touring boot, or ADV boot if you'll ride gravel. Skip short urban and MX boots.

Gloves EN 13594:2015

Essential
  • 1KP or better
  • Leather or tough palm with palm sliders
  • Knuckle armour
  • Wrist strap
  • Stitching on the outside or doubled on the fingers
Nice to have
  • 2KP gauntlet
  • Scaphoid (wrist-bone) protection
  • Little-finger bridge
  • Touchscreen fingertips
  • Separate summer and winter pairs
Luxury
  • Laminated waterproof
  • Heated gloves on the bike's 12 V

Cheap extras

Nice to have
  • Earplugs: wind noise damages hearing at motorway speed
  • Neck tube
  • Merino or synthetic base layer (no cotton)
  • Hi-vis vest
